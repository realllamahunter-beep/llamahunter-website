Origin Story

Grew up on camera

✕

Llama Hunter is a 16-year-old content creator from Niagara Falls, Canada who has been building in the gaming space for half his life.

That sentence sounds like it shouldn't be real—but it is.

He didn't start with a studio, a budget, or industry connections. He started with birthday money and a used PS4 that had already lived a full life before it became part of his story.

But the real beginning isn't the setup—it's the moment he said he wanted to be a streamer. And his dad said yes.

Not as a spectator, but as a partner in the process. Learning the tools. Figuring out livestreams. Sitting through early struggles. Helping him press "go live" for the first time. Building patience in a world that wasn't watching yet.

There was no instant audience. No algorithmic breakthrough. Just consistency—years of showing up, improving, and uploading when almost nobody was there to see it.

That consistency turned into momentum.

By 2019, he had already earned an OG Support-A-Creator code, becoming one of the earliest creators in the program—an early signal of what was coming next.

Today, Llama Hunter has created nearly 2,000 videos, built original shows and content formats, achieved a top 0.01% locker, and even brought his in-game identity into the real world through a custom mascot.

But the numbers are only part of it.

He's still learning. Still refining. Still pushing the edge of what a young creator can build in a constantly changing industry.

And now, after years of quiet consistency, the trajectory is shifting.

Because this isn't the peak of the story.
It's the beginning of the next level.
